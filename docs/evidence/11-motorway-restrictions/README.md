# Speed limits on the gantries, a closed lane, and roadworks

Recorded with `node tools/record_evidence.mjs restrictions` on 2026-10-25: the real application,
stepped at 20 frames a second and captured frame by frame, so each clip plays at true speed unless it says otherwise.
Driven by the Bot lab's reference driver (`js/bots.js`, the one `npm run test:bots` checks every lesson with), which reads the same plan as the examiner: the limit ahead, and lanes about to close. The careless drives are checked by `npm run test:motorway`.

## Limits on the gantries

From the driver's seat: the first gantry shows 60 over every lane, so the driver is down to 60 by the time it gets there; the next shows 50, and an arrow over the left-hand lane. The speed display shows the limit the gantry set.

## Red x and the broken down car

Out of the left-hand lane after the arrow, the driver passes under the red X over that lane, the amber lamps flashing beside it, and then the reason for it: a broken-down car with its hazard lights on. The driver keeps to the middle lane, which the examiner does not call hogging with the lane to its left closed.

[02-red-x-and-the-broken-down-car.mp4](02-red-x-and-the-broken-down-car.mp4) · 34 s · 0.9 MB

## Roadworks lane closed

Roadworks: the half-mile and lane-closed signs, the 50 limit on both sides, then Merge in turn. Overtaking in the right-hand lane when it closes, the driver signals and moves left into a gap in the middle lane before the cones, and keeps to 50 past the works to the end of the roadworks.
