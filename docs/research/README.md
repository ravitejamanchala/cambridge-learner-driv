# Research

- `learner-panel.html` is a simulated panel of 100 learner drivers (all already taking lessons) reacting to v1.1. It covers what they valued, frustrations, requests, a moderated round table, release and pricing (Free / Standard £4.99 / Pro Plus £9), and a rating of all 31 current and planned features. Open it in a browser.
  The personas are written from the app's real feature list and known issues. They are not real users, so treat the findings as hypotheses to check with real learners. Competitor prices were checked on 14 Oct 2026.
