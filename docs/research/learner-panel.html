LCambridge Learner Drive · simulated user panel · 14 Oct 2026

100 learners try the app

A hundred learner drivers, every one of them already taking lessons with an instructor, used the build as it stands today (v1.1, commit 1c5d4f7) and talked through what they would use, what got in the way and what they would ask for next.

These are simulated learners, not real people. I wrote each persona and their reactions from the app's actual feature list and the issues found in testing. Use it to choose what to test with real learners, not as evidence by itself.
