    Controls

    IP
    Engine / parking brake in cockpit lessons
    W↑
    Accelerate
    S↓
    Brake. Automatic lessons: stop, hold the brake and select R to reverse.
    AD
    Steer
    QE
    Signal left / right
    C
    Chase, driver or overhead view
    B
    Interior mirror check. Physical head movement is not measured
    ZX
    Look left / right: a tap is a mirror glance, holding it is a blind-spot check over your shoulder. The camera turns, the car does not
    Esc
    Pause
    Drag
    Pan the route map or overhead view; scroll or pinch to zoom

    Desktop: use the keyboard. Phone or tablet: use the touch buttons in portrait or landscape. Steering and indicators are on the left, brake and accelerator on the right. Hold a pedal and steering button together to drive and turn. Use the on-screen selector for automatic gears.
